Summary (not a substitute for the full Policy). ReChord is built around voice and place. To work, it needs your account details, the voices and images you choose to upload, and your location when you record or browse nearby voices. Public voices and their positions are visible to anyone. We do not sell your personal data, we do not show third-party advertising, and we do not create voiceprints or use your voice to identify you. You can delete a voice or your whole account at any time.

2. The data we collect

2.1 Data you give us

Category What it includes
Account data Email address, username, display name, password (stored in protected form), and a phone number if you choose to add one. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive an identity token and the basic profile information that the provider shares (such as your email address and name).
Profile data Profile photo, voice bio, language preference.
Voices The audio you record and publish, its title, cover image, place name, the visibility you chose (public or private), the time of publication, and its position (see 2.2).

Communications Messages you send to our support, legal or privacy addresses.

2.2 Location data

  • When you record. We collect the position of your device at the time of recording and attach it to the voice. You choose whether the voice is shown at its precise position or at an approximate position.
  • When you browse. We use your current position to show you voices near you and to centre the map. The app sends this position to our servers with the request. The app also keeps your last known position on your device so that the map can open quickly.
  • We do not track your location in the background. The app requests location only while you are using it.
  • You can refuse or withdraw the location permission in your device settings. Without it you cannot publish a voice at your position, and nearby discovery will be limited.

2.3 Data collected automatically

Category What it includes
Device and app data Device model, operating system and version, app version, language and time-zone settings.
Push token The token issued by Apple or Google that lets us send notifications to your device, if you allow notifications.
Log data IP address, date and time of requests, the requests made to our servers, and error information.
Session data An authentication token that keeps you signed in, stored in the protected storage of your device.

2.4 Data that stays on your device

Some things never leave your device unless you decide to share them: recordings you have not published, videos you export from a voice (the video is rendered entirely on your device), cached audio and images, and your theme setting.

2.5 Data about other people

A voice may contain the voice of another person, and a cover image may show another person. You are responsible for having the right to share that material (see the Terms of Use, Section 5.5). A person who believes that they appear in content without permission can contact us at info@rechord.life.

2.6 What we do not collect or do

  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, messages or the photos you have not selected.
  • We do not create voiceprints or biometric templates, and we do not use your voice to identify or authenticate you.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking tools.
  • We do not ask for sensitive data such as health, religion or political opinions. Please do not include such data about yourself or others in your voices unless you are willing to make it public.

4. What other people can see

4.1 Public by design. Your username, display name, profile photo, voice bio and every voice you publish as public (with its title, cover image, place name and position on the map) can be seen and heard by other users.

4.2 Shared links. A public voice can be shared through a link. Anyone who has the link may be able to listen to the voice, including people who do not have a ReChord account.

4.3 Exported videos. A user can export a public voice as a video that shows the creator's name and profile photo and a map of the place, and can share that video outside ReChord.

4.4 Private voices are visible only to you. They are still stored on our servers and may be accessed by authorised staff where that is necessary to investigate a report or a security or legal matter.

4.5 Copies outside the Service. Once content has been shared outside ReChord, we cannot control or delete the copies. Please choose carefully what you publish, in particular a precise position that reveals your home, workplace or routine.

4.6 Likes and notifications. When you like a voice, its creator is told that you did.

4.7 Blocks and reports. A user you block is not told by us that you blocked them. A user you report is not told who filed the report.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share it only as follows.

5.1 Service providers who process data on our instructions: hosting and storage providers, email delivery providers for verification codes, and, where used, crash-reporting providers. LIST OF PROVIDERS AND COUNTRIES

5.2 Platform services that the app uses directly:

  • Maps. The map is provided by Google Maps on Android and by Apple Maps on iOS. The map provider receives the area you are viewing and technical data such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Sign-in. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, that provider processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy.
  • Push notifications. Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google).
  • Map data for video export. When you export a video in a style that draws its own map, the app downloads map data for the surroundings of the voice from OpenStreetMap-based servers. Those servers receive the coordinates of the area and your IP address. They do not receive your account details.

5.3 Other platforms you choose. When you share a link or a video to another app (for example Instagram), that app receives what you share and handles it under its own policy.

5.4 Authorities and legal matters. We may disclose data where we believe in good faith that it is necessary to comply with a law, court order or lawful request, to protect the safety of any person, to report child sexual abuse material or another serious crime, to investigate fraud or a breach of our Terms, or to protect our rights. We assess each request and disclose only what is necessary.

5.5 Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganisation or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5.6 With your consent, or at your direction, in any other case.
