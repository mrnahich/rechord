Why we're building this

Feeds forget. Places don't.

Everything we post slides down a timeline and disappears. But the moments that matter to us happened somewhere: a bench, a doorway, a platform, a kitchen.

We wanted to give those moments back their address. So ReChord has no timeline, no filters and no expiry date. You say it, you pin it, and it stays.
