How it works
01

Say it.

Open ReChord and record up to 60 seconds. No text, no filters, no editing your personality out. A thought, a memory, a little “I was here.”

02

Pin it.

Your voice lands on the exact spot you're standing. Add a title and a photo if you like. Keep it private, or leave it for the world.

03

It stays.

No timeline to sink in. Your voice waits at that place, and anyone exploring the map can find it, listen, and feel what you felt.

The idea

No timelines. No filters. No expiration. Just a voice, left in the place where it happened, waiting for whoever walks by next.
