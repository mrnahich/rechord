The thesis

Three things we believe.

01 · THE GAP

Feeds are full. Places are empty.

Social content has no address. It is consumed once and lost. The physical world, where life actually happens, carries almost none of it.

02 · THE FORMAT

Voice is low effort, high emotion.

Sixty seconds, no camera, no editing. Easier to make than a post, harder to fake, and far more personal to receive.

03 · THE MOAT

Every pin makes the map worth opening.

Voices don't expire, so the map compounds. A place with ten voices is a reason to visit. A city with ten thousand is a layer nobody else has.

How it grows

A loop that runs on real places.

  1. Someone records a momentUp to a minute of voice, right where it happens.
  2. It is pinned to that spotPublic or private, exact or approximate.
  3. Someone else finds it on the mapNearby, or while exploring a city they miss.
  4. They leave a voice of their ownAnd the place becomes a little more alive.
